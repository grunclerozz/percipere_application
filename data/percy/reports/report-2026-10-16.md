# Percy intent classifier — evaluation report (2026-10-16) — PROVISIONAL

Training data: `data/percy/intents.csv` — 1023 rows, 1003 distinct sentences after
preprocessing (20 repeats dropped, 0 dropped for matching the test set).
Fixed test set: `data/percy/test_team.csv` — 58 team-written sentences, never trained on,
never split or reshuffled. Features: TF-IDF word 1–2-grams +
character 3–5-grams (4387 features).
Settings are tuned by stratified 5-fold CV on the training data only.
Exported: `assets/percy/model.json` (version `2026-10-16-1003`).

## Primary: fixed team-only test set
Intents with test sentences: 10 of 32.
Not evaluated (no team test sentences): read, pause, resume, stop, repeat, faster, slower, bigger_text, smaller_text, open_item, greeting, thanks, goodbye, yes, no, out_of_scope, report_issue, describe_image, more_detail, read_image_text, tutorial, set_speed.
